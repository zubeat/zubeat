# Privacy

Zubeat has no accounts, no ads, no tracking and no server of its own. What you play, save and search
for stays on your phone or computer, except as follows.

**To work, the app contacts** YouTube (search, suggestions, songs and covers), MusicBrainz, the Cover Art
Archive, Wikipedia, Wikidata and LRCLIB (titles and artists, to show information and lyrics), Apple (the charts:
the songs most played in the country you look at, which is your own unless you choose another), GitHub
(updates and languages of the app) and PyPI (updates of yt-dlp). They see your IP address and what is
asked for, as with any website. If you open, share or import a link to another service, such as Spotify or Apple Music,
the app also asks that service, or Apple for links from Shazam, what the link points to. Files you import are read on your device;
of videos that a file names only by their IDs, the app asks YouTube for the titles.

**Google Drive, only if you turn it on.** The app stores backups of your playlists, stations, list of saved
songs, listening history, searches, queue and settings, and never the music, as they change, in a hidden folder of your own
Google Drive. The folder belongs to the app: it cannot see any of your other files, and other apps cannot
see the folder. Nobody else receives this data. The app shows the address of your account in its settings
and keeps it on the device. With *Sync between devices*, the same folder also holds one file per phone or computer with your
playlists, stations, list of saved songs, searches and listening history, from which your other devices take over changes.
*Disconnect* in the app's settings stops both. On a computer, you sign in to Google in your web browser; the app
keeps the key it gets for this folder in a file only your user can read. To delete the backups and synced data, open
Google Drive on the web > Settings > Manage apps > Zubeat > Delete hidden app data.

Zubeat's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the
[Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy),
including the Limited Use requirements.

**Android's own backup**, if it is on in your phone's settings, copies the same kind of data to your Google account.
